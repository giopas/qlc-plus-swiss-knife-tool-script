# QLC+ Swiss Knife v3.3.0

The Look & Chaser Builder now works the way the QLC+ 5.3 Show Wizard thinks: groups that say what they can do, effects sorted into families, and buttons on a page per group. Nothing it could do before is gone, and every look still sets every channel of its fixtures.

## One screen, three columns

- **Groups.** A card per group shows its fixtures, what they can do (colour mixing, a colour wheel, gobos, a strobe, movement, a grid for matrices) and a role, one of the wizard's nine, suggested from the group's name.
- **Effects.** Colour, Intensity, Movement and Matrix, as in the wizard. Each effect is a card that says what it makes and on which groups. When none of your groups can do it, it is greyed out and says why.
- **Will add.** The stage seen from above, lit with your looks, the counts and the Doctor's verdict as you tick, and where the buttons go.

**Start from** Club night, Concert, Theatre or Architectural ticks the effects the wizard would tick for that kind of show and gives each one to the groups whose role suits it. Change anything after.

## New looks and effects

- Gobo looks: one per gobo and colour. You pick the gobos; the card says how many looks that makes.
- Full, Half and Off, and Strobe slow and fast. The strobe uses each fixture's own strobe range, so it also works on fixtures where the wizard's strobe palettes do nothing.
- Blinder hit, on a flash button.
- Places on the stage: the centre, the audience and each musician you placed in Stage & Meshes. Your moving heads' looks aim there.
- Movement effects, as the wizard makes them: circle, figure eight and audience sweep ride on the look that is playing; fly out and fly in set the aim themselves. They work in QLC+ 5.2.2 too.
- Matrices on any group laid out as a grid, not only on pixel bars.

## On the console

By default the buttons go on one page with a frame and a page per group, like the wizard's: an intensity slider, the group's looks (one at a time), its effects, PANIC RESET and an XY pad for the moving heads. One page with every button, or no buttons, are still options.

## Chasers

A chaser opens in its own panel: recipe, pattern, colours, speed with Tap, cut or soft fade, and a large preview with ▶ Play. The expert settings are all still there, under More.

Claude can build all of it too (`build_looks`).
