      Sessions

      Build, adapt, check and document your QLC+ 5 shows

      QLC+ Swiss Knife reads your QLC+ workspace (.qxw) and does the long, fiddly jobs for you. Open a show and it becomes the show in progress: every tool works on it, one after the other, as often as you need — the header counts the changes, the History lets you go back to any step, and 💾 Save as new file… writes it all (name_v2.qxw + a report). Your original is never touched. The six groups below are the same 1–6 groups of the menu on the left.

      1
      New show
      Start from your fixtures: a ready-to-run show with looks, effects, PANIC RESET and a Virtual Console — or place and patch the rig by hand.
      2
      Adapt a show
      Take a show to a smaller or different rig: keep the fixtures you have, bring functions across from another show, even the brightness out.
      3
      Create
      Build looks and chasers, lay out the Virtual Console, place the 3D meshes and fixtures on the stage.
      4
      Run the show
      Tonight’s setlist into a CueList, keyboard and MIDI bindings without clashes, a description of every function.
      5
      Check & fix
      Broken references, channels left unset, strobe left on, a PANIC RESET that can’t reset — found and fixed; every ID in a table.
      6
      Document
      The paper for each reader, in one tool: tech rider for the venue, crew checklist for load-in, show book with decoded DMX values for you — PDF or CSV.
