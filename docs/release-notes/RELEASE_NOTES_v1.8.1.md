# QLC+ Swiss Knife v1.8.1 — meshes around fixtures

### New
- **Stage & Meshes**: fixtures join the placement tools. They are drawn with their real size, can be selected, dragged and nudged, and with meshes and fixtures selected together you choose who moves — the others are the reference:
  - a mesh **exactly between two fixtures** (centres ↔) or at equal gaps (space evenly);
  - a fixture **right above a musician** (centres ↔ and ↕);
  - floor PARs lined up with the risers.
  A single fixture can be moved by its centre and height; its tilt and pan stay as they are.

Your original files are never changed: every result is a new `.qxw`, with a report next to it.

Full details: [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md). Wiki: [Stage and Meshes](https://github.com/giopas/qlc-plus-swiss-knife-tool-script/wiki/Stage-and-Meshes).
