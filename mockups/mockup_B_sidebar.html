What is QLC+ Swiss Knife?

A free companion toolkit for QLC+ 5.x show files. It opens your .qxw workspace in the browser and helps with everything QLC+ itself doesn't: building setlists from your functions, printing patch checklists and stage blueprints, documenting what every scene does, auditing key & MIDI triggers, tidying the Virtual Console layout, balancing fixture brightness, and merging content between shows. Your original files are never overwritten — every generator writes a new file.

Independent community project — not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the QLC+ project or its development team.
