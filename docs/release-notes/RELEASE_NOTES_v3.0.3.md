# QLC+ Swiss Knife v3.0.3

A follow-up to 3.0.2 after a first look at the new languages on the Mac.

## Language

- The tool names are now translated in every language, Italian and French included. *Quick Start* is *Avvio rapido*, *クイックスタート* or *Schnellstart*, and the sentences that mention a tool use the same name. Links to the wiki keep the English page names.
- The Japanese menu no longer reads *ファンクション Porter*.
- The Look Builder shows the palette colours in your language. The fixture groups listed there keep the names they have in your show: a group called *Floor* no longer shows as *Boden* in German.
- Text that was still English in every language is translated: *Console* in the guided routes, several Doctor titles and the D012 message, the Look Builder patterns, positions and presets, the CueList actions, the placeholders of text boxes and the tooltip of the language box.

Full details: [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/giopas/qlc-plus-swiss-knife-tool-script/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
