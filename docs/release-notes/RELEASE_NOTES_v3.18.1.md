### Added — the tablet follows the setlist QLC+ plays (3.18.1)

- **The tablet shows the cue list that starts playing.** Put several setlists on the page (20Minutes, Feed The Fox, Strange Traffic…): when one starts in QLC+, from a button, a pedal or the keyboard, the tablet switches to it by itself, not only when a cue moves. Its tab gets a green ▶. Right after the tablet connects, it stays on the list it shows while that one plays.
- **Add them to the page.** When the show has cue lists that are not on the page yet, *Tablet on stage* names them with one button that puts them all on the page.
- **Open here** in QLC+ Stage Monitor: next to each address, it opens the page (or one player's view) in this computer's browser.
- **Colours on the tablet itself.** A ◐ button in the page's corner goes through Dark, Light, High contrast, Black with a white song and Black with a grey song; each tablet keeps its choice. **Light** is new, also in Swiss Knife's *Colours*.

### Changed (3.18.1)

- **The keep-awake button is an eye** (open: the screen stays on; crossed out: it may sleep). ☀/☾ looked like a light/dark switch.
- **No empty space above the first song.** The list starts right under the header; later songs still scroll to the middle.
- **Stop in QLC+ Stage Monitor is red**, so it is easy to find.
