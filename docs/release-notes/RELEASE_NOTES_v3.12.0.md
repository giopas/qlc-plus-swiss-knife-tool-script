### Added — Brightness levels (3.12.0)

- **Where the scenes usually stop.** Each card and each fixture now says where nine lit scenes in ten stop (*Usually up to 90%*), next to the brightest scene. A level that was chosen long ago shows up even when a few scenes go higher.
- **Brightest scene to a level.** Type a level, for example 75%, on a card or for every card at once. **Scale** moves the slider so the brightest scene lands there and keeps the differences between scenes. **Limit** brings down only the scenes above the level and leaves the dimmer ones as they are. The card says where the brightest scene ends up.
- A limit is kept in the recipe and shown in the history box of later versions (*limit 90%*).
- The wiki and all seven languages are updated.
