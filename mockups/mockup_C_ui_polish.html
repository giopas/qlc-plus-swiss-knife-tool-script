    What this mockup shows (implemented on the real screens):
    • Ribbon: labelled counts and status cards (Doctor in amber/red/green, unsaved changes) instead of bare numbers.
    • Sidebar: groups that read as groups; the History always visible with ✓ steps and ↶.
    • Start: guided routes first, with their steps; the six jobs as a compact index under them.
    • Every tool: the footer says what Apply will do, next to the button; the Reducer computes it as you tick.
    • Tables: zebra, hover, quiet ID column, the name stands out. ⌘K / Ctrl+K: go to any tool or action.
