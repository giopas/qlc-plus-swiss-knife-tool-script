# QLC+ Swiss Knife v3.4.0

A guided route used to have two ways forward: *Done, next* at the top and the tool's own *Next* at the bottom, with two step counters that looked the same. Now there is one.

## The top shows where you are

- The route strip shows *step 3 of 8* and each step done, current, skipped or still to do.
- The current step opens into a second row, like a tab: the steps of the tool you are in (Quick Start, Function Porter) or the panel you have open (an effect in the Look Builder).
- *Skip this step* and ✕ are the only buttons up there. They leave a step; they never finish it.

## The footer moves you on

- Inside a tool, its button says where it goes: *Next: Placement →*.
- When the route step is done, the footer's main button is *Go on: 4 Console ›*, and *Finish the route* at the end.
- At the end of Quick Start, *Save and go on: 2 Groups ›* saves the new show, opens it and ticks the step.
- In the Look Builder's effect panel, *Cancel* and *Keep these settings* are in the footer, and there is only one *Done*-like button on screen.

Outside a route, Quick Start and Function Porter show their steps with the same stepper. Nothing changes in what any tool writes in the show.
