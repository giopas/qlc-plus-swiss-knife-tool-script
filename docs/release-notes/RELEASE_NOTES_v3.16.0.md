### Added — your decisions kept in Cabling (3.16.0)

- **Fixtures you send by radio stay by radio.** *Propose* used to start from scratch, so a fixture you had put on a wireless receiver went back on a cable. Now every alternative keeps your fixtures by radio, each with its receiver, from one transmitter on a free output, and cables the other fixtures around them. The note of each alternative says how many it kept.
- **Approve this plan.** Over the plan, *Approve this plan* keeps it as the one you want, with the date, next to the show. While nothing changes, the bar says *Approved plan*. After a change (an alternative picked, a fixture moved) it says *Changed since you approved it*, with *Approve the changes* and *Back to the approved plan*.
- **Propose again to check.** With an approved plan, each alternative says how it compares: more or less cable, errors and warnings. Picking one never loses the approved plan: *Back to the approved plan* brings it back.
- After a change by hand, the list of alternatives says to propose again.
